Let's make some movies

"As long as it's interesting anything goes in movies" - Togata

I am always hungry to learn and grow my skills, I have a passion for cooking due to it being the backbone of human connection. For my 25th birthday I want to do a film festival on 3/20 at bren mar park, I am already working on my own film but I think it would be cool to expand this into a community event to showcase the talents and dreams of all my friends in the area. If you are interested in making or showcasing a film please email me below with a google drive or unlisted youtube video link to your film. I already have a projector and I plan on renting out those big inflatable screens as well as bbqing all day so I think this can be a real fun time for everyone!

Offical rules

All films are approved by me beforehand and I will not show any films that are overtly hateful in anyways, you are welcome to make mature and dark films, but I have no love or respect for things made from spite. Film length is at 40 minutes maximum unless you have a really great film then I can make an exception, but overall I am looking for short films. All forms of film are accepted (live action, animation, clay mation and collage there is no limit to your vision) lastly if you are showcasing a film please also stay for the duration of the festival as to support fellow artists.
